# Changelog

## 1.4.0

- Added crafting recipes for the Fat Man, Mini-Nuke and Napalm Bomb

## 1.3.3

- Redrew the Mini-Nuke as a finned bomb
- The Fat Man's loading frames now show the Mini-Nuke's nose sliding in

## 1.3.2

- The Fat Man now shows a Mini-Nuke sliding into the muzzle as you load it

## 1.3.1

- Napalm Bombs now set the ground on fire properly, and the flames keep burning for about 10 seconds

## 1.3.0

- The Fat Man has its own sounds instead of the bow sound: a heavy clunk when loading, a click at full power, and a thump, whoosh and echo when firing
- The launcher now trembles while loading and kicks back when it fires, with a small camera shake and a puff of smoke

## 1.2.0

- Mini-Nukes no longer start fires. Fire is now only the Napalm Bomb's job
- The Mini-Nuke now has a mushroom cloud
- The settings toggle is now called **Napalm Bombs start fires**

## 1.1.0

- New Napalm Bomb ammo: a small blast that sets mobs and players within 8 blocks on fire and spreads flames over the ground
- Operators run `/thecoberto:fatman` to choose whether explosions break blocks and start fires
- Requires Minecraft 1.21.100 or newer
